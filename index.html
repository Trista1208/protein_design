One seed is not a score

Two of the scored terms are pass or fail. The aspartate to ligand contact and the glutamine to ligand contact both have to land between 2.0 and 3.6 Angstrom, at an approach angle of about 130 degrees; outside that the term is zero, and the score multiplies to zero with it. On top of that sit the two hard gates: at most 260 residues and no more than 70 percent identity to a published enzyme. That is why we check whether the ligand is actually bound before reading any geometry, and why a single seed is never a score. Most of day two went into measurement rather than design.

Ninety seconds. Walk left to right: the theozyme is the fixed motif, RFdiffusion3 grows a backbone around it and we drop anything with a chain break, a ligand clash, or a catalytic residue within 5 Angstrom of a terminus. LigandMPNN writes the sequence with Asp and Gln locked, sweeping temperature from 0.1 to 0.5. Boltz-2 folds five seeds and each one is scored alone. Then the loop along the bottom: de novo scaffolds, single pocket mutations, paired mutations, with the winners of each round becoming the parent of the next. No HG3, no KE07, no published Kemp fold as a starting point.

CLICK OR DROP

The core of the talk. All five seeds clear the contact window, so we quote this as 0.887, five of five — never as an average. The dashed bar is HG3.17, a published crystal structure, through the same pipeline at 0.959. Our own de novo models are the path that got us here, not the result: kemp_E52Q places both contacts at 2.68 and 2.76 Angstrom but on one Boltz sample out of three, and kemp_C21A sits at 4.86, outside the window. Neither carries a five-seed score.

CLICK OR DROP

A score is a filter, not a rate constant.

Last thirty seconds. Two points and stop. We submit five designs that are one scaffold with different pocket packing, chosen for holding across seeds rather than for the top number. And we are not yet claiming the identity and TM gates: the submission file marks the sequences eligible but its identity field reads 0.0 against an empty target, and the recorded gate is length only. We run both before we claim under 70 percent and under 0.7. Then close on the caveat.
