One seed is not a score

Two of the scored terms are pass or fail. The aspartate contact and the glutamine contact both have to land between 2.0 and 3.6 Angstrom; outside that band the term is zero, and the score multiplies to zero with it. That is why we check whether the ligand is actually bound before reading any geometry, and why a single seed is never a score. Most of day two went into measurement rather than design.

Ninety seconds. We generated de novo scaffolds and got one that folds at 0.98 confidence, but its pocket was not reproducible across seeds. The win was to freeze that backbone and change only one to three pocket residues, so the glutamine rotamer stops swinging between seeds. I23V plus M45Y is the pair that holds on all five. No HG3, no KE07, no published Kemp fold as a starting point.
